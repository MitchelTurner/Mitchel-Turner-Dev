Shoal water

Nobody decides to buy software. They notice the leak.

If two or more of these sound like your week, it's worth a conversation.

  • The same information gets typed into three places, and by Friday they disagree.
  • One spreadsheet runs a critical part of the business, and exactly one person understands it.
  • Invoicing, deposits, or payroll eat a full day every month that nobody has.
  • You pay for software that does 20% of what you need and blocks the other 80%.
  • Two systems can't talk to each other, so a person is the integration.
  • You can't answer a basic question about last season without rebuilding the numbers by hand.
  • Growth means hiring another admin instead of selling more.

A 45-minute call will tell you whether custom software is worth it — or whether an off-the-shelf tool you haven't tried yet does the job for a fraction of the price. I'll say so if it does.
